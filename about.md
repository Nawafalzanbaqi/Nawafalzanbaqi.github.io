# 👤 About Me

I’m **Nawaf Alzanbaqi**, a Full-Stack Developer in training based in Riyadh,  
with a BSc in **Information Systems** and hands-on experience at **Tuwaiq Bootcamp**.

I focus on:

- Building full-stack web applications using **React, JavaScript, Node.js, and Python**  
- Designing **simple, clear UI/UX** that helps users get things done  
- Writing **maintainable, structured code** and keeping repositories clean  
- Using **Git & GitHub** with proper branches, pull requests, and reviews  
- Applying **data analysis** (Python/SQL) to understand and improve products  

My goal is to become a developer who can **ship real features**, communicate clearly,  
and add value to teams from day one.

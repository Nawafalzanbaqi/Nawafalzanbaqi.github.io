# Daily Learning Log 📅

I track what I learn every day in this repository:

👉 **Daily Learning:**  
<https://github.com/Nawafalzanbaqi/Daily-Learning>

Each entry is short and simple:

- Topic  
- What I learned  
- Time spent  

This helps me stay consistent and makes my progress visible over time.

---

## Why I keep a daily log

- To build the habit of **showing up every day**  
- To see how my skills grow week by week  
- To have a clear history of what I studied and practiced  

If you’re checking this page, feel free to look at the repo and see how my learning evolves over time.
